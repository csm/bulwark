The native input module adapts the keyboard and pointer conversion from Servo 0.7.0,
`examples/winit_minimal.rs`, under MPL-2.0, via Bulwark's E3 and E7 experiments.

Source: <https://github.com/servo/servo/blob/v0.7.0/components/servo/examples/winit_minimal.rs>

The original license notice is retained in `src/native/input.rs`.
